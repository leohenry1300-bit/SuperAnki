## Faux amis | 🪤
actually ;; en fait (≠ actuellement) ;; Actually, I disagree. → Actuellement = currently
currently ;; actuellement ;; She is currently abroad.
eventually ;; finalement, à la fin (≠ éventuellement) ;; He eventually found a job. → éventuellement = possibly
possibly ;; éventuellement, peut-être ;;
to attend ;; assister à (≠ attendre) ;; Attend the meeting. → attendre = to wait
to wait for ;; attendre ;; I'm waiting for the bus.
a college ;; une université / un établissement supérieur (≠ collège) ;; She studies at college.
a lecture ;; un cours magistral, une conférence (≠ lecture) ;; → lecture (lire) = reading
a library ;; une bibliothèque (≠ librairie) ;; → librairie = bookstore

to demand ;; exiger (≠ demander) ;; The union demands a raise. → demander = to ask
to ask ;; demander ;;
a sensible person ;; une personne raisonnable (≠ sensible) ;; → sensible = sensitive
sensitive ;; sensible, délicat ;;
a journey ;; un voyage (trajet) (≠ journée) ;; a long journey → journée = day
a day ;; une journée ;;
to pretend ;; faire semblant (≠ prétendre) ;; → prétendre = to claim
to claim ;; prétendre, réclamer ;; He claims to be an expert.
to resume ;; reprendre, recommencer (≠ résumer) ;; Work will resume Monday. → résumer = to summarize
a résumé ;; un CV (US) ;;
to rest ;; se reposer (≠ rester) ;; → rester = to stay
to stay ;; rester ;; Stay here.
a car ;; une voiture (≠ car de transport) ;; → car = a coach / a bus

a rental ;; une location ;;

a blessing ;; une bénédiction (≠ blessure) ;; → blessure = injury / wound

a corner ;; un coin ;;
a figure ;; un chiffre (≠ figure) ;; sales figures = chiffres de vente
a fabric ;; un tissu (≠ fabrique) ;; → fabrique = factory

a preservative ;; un conservateur (≠ préservatif) ;;
a delicate matter ;; une affaire délicate (≠ délicieux) ;; → délicieux = delicious

## Erreurs classiques des francophones | 🇫🇷
I am 25 / I have 25 years ;; I am 25 (years old) ;; On dit « be » pour l'âge, jamais « have ».
I am agree ;; I agree ;; to agree = verbe, pas d'auxiliaire « be ».
I am here since Monday ;; I have been here since Monday ;; Depuis → present perfect (since / for).
He explained me the problem ;; He explained the problem to me ;; to explain something to someone.
Discuss about the budget ;; Discuss the budget ;; discuss se construit sans préposition.
Consider about it ;; Consider it ;; consider se construit sans préposition.
I will call you when I will arrive ;; I will call you when I arrive ;; Après when, as soon as, before, after, until : présent.
I look forward to see you ;; I look forward to seeing you ;; look forward to + -ing.
I have 2 sisters. Their are nice. ;; They are nice. ;; their = possessif ; they're = they are ; there = là.
The informations are useful ;; The information is useful ;; information est indénombrable (pas de -s).
Some advices ;; Some advice / a piece of advice ;; advice, news, furniture, luggage, equipment = indénombrables.
It depends of ;; It depends on ;;
Married with ;; married to ;;
Since 3 years ;; for 3 years ;;
Make a photo ;; take a photo ;;
Take a decision ;; make a decision ;;
Hair are long ;; Hair is long ;; hair : singulier (une chevelure).
People is / The people is ;; People are ;; people = pluriel.
He work here ;; He works here ;; 3e personne du singulier : -s.
Do you can help me? ;; Can you help me? ;; can n'accepte pas do.
I can to swim ;; I can swim ;; pas de « to » après can.
More cheap ;; cheaper ;; adjectif court : -er.
The most cheap ;; the cheapest ;;
I want that you come ;; I want you to come ;; want + objet + to + verbe.
I must to go ;; I must go ;;
