## Phrasal verbs courants | 🧩
to set up ;; mettre en place, créer ;; They set up a new branch.
to turn down ;; refuser (une offre) / baisser (son) ;; She turned down the job offer.
to turn up ;; arriver / monter (le son) ;; He turned up late.
to call off ;; annuler ;; The match was called off.
to put off ;; remettre à plus tard ;; Don't put off the decision.
to look into ;; examiner, enquêter sur ;; We'll look into the problem.
to look for ;; chercher ;; I'm looking for my keys.
to look after ;; s'occuper de ;; She looks after the accounts.
to find out ;; découvrir, se renseigner ;; I'll find out the price.
to figure out ;; comprendre, trouver la solution ;; We figured out the error.
to point out ;; faire remarquer ;; He pointed out a mistake.
to come up with ;; trouver (une idée) ;; She came up with a great plan.
to take over ;; reprendre, racheter ;; A larger firm took over the company.
to take off ;; décoller / enlever ;; The plane took off at noon.
to take on ;; embaucher, prendre en charge ;; We took on five new workers.
to work out ;; résoudre, calculer / se passer bien ;; It worked out well.
to fill in / to fill out ;; remplir (un formulaire) ;; Please fill out this form.
to hand in ;; remettre (un devoir, un dossier) ;; Hand in your report by 5.
to hand out ;; distribuer ;; He handed out brochures.
to pick up ;; récupérer, ramasser ;; I'll pick you up at the airport.
to drop off ;; déposer ;; Drop off the package at the desk.
to sign up for ;; s'inscrire à ;; Sign up for the newsletter.
to run out of ;; manquer de, ne plus avoir de ;; We ran out of paper.
to cut back on ;; réduire ;; The firm cut back on spending.
to catch up with ;; rattraper ;; I need to catch up with my emails.
to keep up with ;; suivre le rythme ;; It's hard to keep up with technology.
to get along with ;; s'entendre avec ;; She gets along with her colleagues.
to get back to ;; rappeler / revenir vers ;; I'll get back to you tomorrow.
to get over ;; surmonter ;; He got over the loss.
to rely on ;; compter sur ;; You can rely on me.
to bring up ;; aborder (un sujet), élever ;; He brought up the budget.
to bring about ;; provoquer ;; The change brought about growth.
to go over ;; réviser, examiner ;; Let's go over the figures.
to go ahead ;; aller de l'avant ;; The project will go ahead.
to back up ;; sauvegarder, soutenir ;; Back up your data.
to break down ;; tomber en panne ;; The copier broke down.
to wrap up ;; conclure ;; Let's wrap up the meeting.
to sum up ;; résumer ;; To sum up, we need more time.
to step down ;; démissionner d'un poste ;; The CEO stepped down.
to phase out ;; supprimer progressivement ;; The model will be phased out.
to roll out ;; déployer ;; They rolled out the new system.
to end up ;; finir par ;; We ended up staying late.
to point to ;; indiquer ;; The data points to growth.
to account for ;; représenter, expliquer ;; Exports account for 40% of sales.
to result in ;; entraîner, avoir pour résultat ;; The error resulted in a delay.
to stem from ;; provenir de ;; The delay stems from a shortage.
to give up ;; abandonner ;; Never give up.
to look up ;; chercher (dans un livre, en ligne) ;; Look up the word in a dictionary.
to show up ;; se présenter ;; He didn't show up.
to hold on ;; patienter ;; Hold on a second.
to pass on ;; transmettre ;; Please pass on my regards.
to follow up on ;; assurer le suivi ;; I'll follow up on that.
to sort out ;; régler, résoudre ;; We'll sort out the problem.
to live up to ;; être à la hauteur de ;; The product lived up to expectations.
C: We need to {{c1::set up}} a meeting. ;; set up = organiser, mettre en place
C: They decided to {{c1::call off}} the event. ;; call off = annuler
C: Please {{c1::fill out}} the form. ;; fill out / fill in = remplir
C: He {{c1::turned down}} the offer. ;; turn down = refuser
C: The plane {{c1::took off}} on time. ;; take off = décoller
C: We have {{c1::run out of}} toner. ;; run out of = ne plus avoir de
C: I'll {{c1::get back to}} you. ;; get back to = revenir vers qqn
